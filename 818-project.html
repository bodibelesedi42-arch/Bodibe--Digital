818 Khangeziwe Services

Modern website design for a mobile services business

Client818 Khangeziwe Services
IndustryMobile & Event Services
ServicesWebsite Design & Development
LocationSoweto, South Africa

Project Overview

818 Khangeziwe Services is a Soweto-based mobile services business providing practical solutions for events, businesses and customers.

The Challenge

The business had no professional online presence, making it harder for potential customers to discover their services or find a reliable way to get in touch.

The Approach

We designed a clean, mobile-friendly business website structured around clear service presentation and easy contact — prioritising straightforward navigation over decoration, so visitors can find what they need quickly.

The Goal

The goal was to create a professional online presence that clearly presents the company's services and makes it easier for potential customers to discover and contact the business.

What We Built

  • Responsive business website
  • Modern and professional interface
  • Mobile-friendly layout
  • Clear service presentation
  • Easy navigation
  • Strong call-to-action sections

Result

818 Khangeziwe Services now has a live, professional website that presents their services clearly and gives customers a direct way to get in touch — replacing having no web presence at all.
